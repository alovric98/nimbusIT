NIMBUSIT / PRIVACY

Your enquiry.
Your information.

This notice explains how information is used when you visit nimbusit.hr or send an enquiry. For questions about your data, contact Antonio Lovrić, NimbusIT, at info.nimbusit@gmail.com or +385 98 915 7800.

What information do I use?

The contact form asks for your name, email address and message. You can also add a business name or website. If you choose a package, that choice is sent with your enquiry.

I use this information to reply and discuss possible work with you, including taking steps at your request before entering into a contract. Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to a newsletter or promotional emails.

Which services are involved?

The form is sent through Web3Forms (privacy policy, opens in a new tab) and delivered to my contact email. Direct email correspondence uses Gmail. Cloudflare hosts the site and may process technical request data, such as your IP address, to deliver and protect it.

These providers may process data outside the European Economic Area under their own terms and applicable safeguards.

How long do I keep it?

I keep enquiry information for as long as it is needed to reply and discuss possible work. If we work together, some information may also be needed to carry out the contract or meet legal obligations. Information that is no longer needed is deleted, unless it must be retained by law.

Cookies and browser storage

This version of the site does not load Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or advertising tags. Fonts and photographs are served from this site.

This version stores no browser preferences. The old cookie-notice choice is removed on the next visit with JavaScript enabled. See the cookie policy.

External websites have their own privacy and cookie policies.

Your rights

You can request access to your personal data, corrections, deletion or restrictions on processing. Where applicable, you can also request data portability or object to processing. Email info.nimbusit@gmail.com to make a request.

You also have the right to complain to the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency, AZOP (opens in a new tab).
