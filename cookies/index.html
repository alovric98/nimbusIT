NIMBUSIT / COOKIES & STORAGE

A quick look
at what’s stored.

This site does not use analytics or advertising tracking. The only browser preference it saves is your request to close the cookie notice.

What does this version use?

NimbusIT does not set its own HTTP cookies or load Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or advertising tags. Fonts and photographs are served locally.

Choosing “Got it, remember” saves your request to close the notice in your browser’s local storage. It is not consent to analytics or advertising. No new local-storage entry is created before you make that choice.

The storage entry

Name
nimbusit.cookie-notice.v1
Type
Browser local storage (localStorage), rather than an HTTP cookie. It is not sent to the server with each request.
Purpose
To remember your request not to show the notice on every visit.
Contents
The notice version and the time it was closed. No form data or tracking identifier.
Duration
The choice is valid for 180 days. An expired entry is removed the next time you open the site, and the notice appears again. You can delete it earlier.
Who uses it?
NimbusIT, in your browser on this domain only. The entry is not shared with advertising or analytics services.

Changing your choice

You can reopen the notice from any page footer. Use the button below to delete the saved choice, or clear this site’s data in your browser settings. The site and contact form still work if browser storage is blocked; the notice may appear again.

The contact form and external services

Web3Forms is contacted only when you submit the enquiry form. The privacy notice explains the use of contact and technical data. External links take you to websites with their own policies.

If tracking is added later

Analytics and advertising tools are not enabled in this version. If they are introduced, I will ask for separate consent before activating them, with equally straightforward ways to decline and withdraw that choice. Closing this notice does not approve future tracking.
