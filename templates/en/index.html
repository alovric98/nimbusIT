05 / FAQ

Before you
get in touch.

Do I need a new website?+

Not necessarily. I can improve your existing content, add a landing page or build a configurator or CRM connection. I’ll look at what’s worth keeping before recommending a rebuild.

How long will it take?+

That depends on the scope, content and integrations. Your quote includes a schedule and the materials I need from you. I plan the build once those are agreed.

Are ads and ongoing management included?+

Website & ads and Website & custom tools include the initial setup of one ad channel within the listed scope. Ongoing management is a monthly service. You pay ad spend directly to the platforms; hosting, licences and external tools are separate.

Can you guarantee rankings or enquiries?+

No. Your offer, market, budget and how you respond to enquiries all affect the result. I agree specific deliverables, set up measurement and use the available data to decide what to improve.

What do I get at handover?+

The finished website, documentation and agreed access. Your domain and ad accounts are set up in your name. I explain any third-party licences or restrictions before you commit.
